# FD-11: Merge duplicate tickets

**Size:** L

## Background

Customers often write twice about the same thing: once from the web form and once from their phone, or a second time because they didn't hear back. Each email becomes its own ticket, two teammates pick them up, and the customer gets two different answers. Teammates need to merge duplicates into one ticket.

## Acceptance criteria

1. From a ticket's page, a teammate can merge that ticket into another one by entering the other ticket's number.
2. `POST /api/tickets/{id}/merge` with `{ "intoTicketId": number }` merges ticket `id` into ticket `intoTicketId` and responds with the surviving ticket, in the same shape as `GET /api/tickets/{id}`.
3. The surviving ticket shows every message from both tickets in one thread, oldest first.
4. The merged ticket no longer appears in any ticket list, whatever the filter.
5. Tags from both tickets end up on the surviving ticket.
6. The surviving ticket keeps the right assignee.
7. Merging doesn't reset the customer's SLA clock.
8. Only tickets from the same customer can be merged.
9. Teammates can merge tickets that came from different addresses at the same company.
10. Merging a ticket into itself is rejected with `400`. An unknown ticket on either side gets `404`.
11. Replies to either original conversation end up on the surviving ticket.
12. Merging can't be undone, so the web app asks the teammate to confirm first.
