# FD-04: Pending-customer pauses the SLA clock

**Size:** M

## Background

"Waiting on customer" moves a ticket to `pending`. Teammates expect that to stop the SLA clock, because the delay is the customer's, not ours. It doesn't. Pending tickets keep counting down and then show as overdue. Teammates have also reported that the badge is sometimes just wrong after a ticket is paused or closed, and fixes itself later.

The SLA is measured in business time. See `docs/architecture.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. While a ticket is `pending`, its SLA state is `paused`. This is a new value of the SLA `state` in the API contract.
2. A paused ticket's `remainingMinutes` stays at the business minutes it had left when it went pending, however long it stays pending. A paused ticket is never `breached`.
3. When a pending ticket moves to `open` or `closed`, its due time moves later by the business minutes it spent pending. Time spent pending outside business hours adds nothing.
4. A ticket can be paused any number of times, and the pauses add up.
5. Every status change shows up in the SLA on the very next read, from `GET /api/tickets` and `GET /api/tickets/{id}` alike, and in the response to the status change itself.
6. In the web app, a paused ticket's SLA badge starts with the word "Paused", in the inbox and on the ticket page, instead of "Due in…" or "Overdue…".
7. The OpenAPI contract and the generated types include the new state.
